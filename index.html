Known limits

stated plainly, not buried
[LIMIT] Only finds photos that are already indexed somewhere online — it cannot identify someone with no public photo footprint.
[LIMIT] Search accuracy depends on Google's index; a genuine match can occasionally be missed or ranked low.
[LIMIT] Verification confirms a candidate photo shows the same face — it does not confirm the person's real-world identity beyond what the source page claims.
[SCOPE] Runs on Polygon Amoy, a public testnet — chosen for a zero-cost, fully reproducible demo. The same contract works unmodified on mainnet.
